# Pensiv Pensieve

**Pensiv Pensieve** is an all-in-one productivity and personal management application designed to help you organize your tasks, habits, goals, notes, and daily life in one place.

✨ Features

✅ **Tasks & To-Do List — Create, organize, and manage your daily tasks
🔄 Habits — Build and track your habits and routines
📅 Work Calendar — Plan and organize your schedule
🎵 Music — Listen to music while working or studying
⏱️ Focus Timer — Stay focused with built-in productivity timers
📝 Notes — Quickly write down and organize your ideas
🎯 Goals — Set and track your personal goals
📚 Books — Keep track of the books you want to read and your reading progress
🤖 AI Assistant — Get help and interact with an integrated AI assistant
🎨 Themes — Customize the application with different themes**

Pensiv Pensieve brings the tools you need for **productivity, planning, focus, and personal organization** together in a single application.
